ABOUT VLDB

Research across borders.

The VLDB Endowment promotes the exchange of scholarly work in databases and related fields worldwide.

It steers the VLDB conference series, collaborates with PVLDB on the conference review process, publishes The VLDB Journal with Springer, and supports scholarly activities and training.
